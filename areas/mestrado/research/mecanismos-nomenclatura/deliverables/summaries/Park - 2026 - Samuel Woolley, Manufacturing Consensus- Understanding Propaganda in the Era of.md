# Review of Samuel Woolley, Manufacturing Consensus: Understanding Propaganda in the Era of Automation and Anonymity (Yale University Press, 2023)

- **Citation key**: Park_2026
- **Objective**: To review Woolley's (2023) book on computational propaganda and the concept of manufacturing consensus.
- **Method**: Book review. Note that this summary is based on the review, not on the book itself.
- **Key findings**:
  - According to the reviewer, Woolley's core thesis is that automation and anonymity enabled a new mode of manipulation, "manufacturing consensus", a riff on Herman and Chomsky's "manufacturing consent".
  - Manufacturing consensus creates an "illusion of popularity" that triggers bandwagon effects through perceived social pressure.
  - Woolley adds a sixth filter to the propaganda model (automated and anonymous manipulation of social media) and describes three levels: tool-based, algorithm-based, and media-based legitimation.
  - The book draws on over eight years of fieldwork with bot builders and political strategists.
- **Relevance to theme**: Secondary evidence that "manufactured consensus" has a defining source by a major author and press, which bears on whether FAMO mechanism G3 keeps its name. Woolley's own text was not read.
