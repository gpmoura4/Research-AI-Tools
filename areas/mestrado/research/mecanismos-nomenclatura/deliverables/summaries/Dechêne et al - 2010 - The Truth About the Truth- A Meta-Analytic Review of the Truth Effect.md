# The Truth About the Truth: A Meta-Analytic Review of the Truth Effect

- **Citation key**: Dechene_2010
- **Objective**: To meta-analyse the repetition-induced truth effect and compare its two measurement criteria: within-items (truth gain from first to second encounter) and between-items (repeated versus new statements).
- **Method**: Meta-analysis of 51 studies of the repetition-induced truth effect, with moderator analyses.
- **Key findings**:
  - The between-items effect is larger than the within-items effect.
  - Several moderators affect the two effects differently, suggesting different comparison processes underlie them.
  - The results are interpreted within the processing-fluency account of the truth effect.
  - The review traces the effect to early studies including Hasher et al. (1977) and Begg et al. (1992).
- **Relevance to theme**: Consolidates the "truth effect" label used as the alternative name of FAMO mechanism C7 and provides a defining review beyond the original 1977 demonstration.
- **Keywords**: truth effect; meta-analysis; processing fluency
