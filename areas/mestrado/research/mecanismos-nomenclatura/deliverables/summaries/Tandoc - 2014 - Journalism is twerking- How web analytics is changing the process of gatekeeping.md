# Journalism is twerking? How web analytics is changing the process of gatekeeping

- **Citation key**: Tandoc_2014
- **Objective**: To explain how and why journalists integrate audience feedback from web analytics into news work, using field theory.
- **Method**: Case studies of three online newsrooms, with about 150 hours of observation and 30 interviews.
- **Key findings**:
  - Journalists treat the audience as economic capital under declining revenues, which opens the gates to audience influence through web analytics, "a new mechanism for audience feedback".
  - Editors used metrics mainly to raise traffic: promoting stories that perform well and predicting which topics and headlines would generate traffic.
  - The study identifies a new gatekeeping process, de-selection (removing stories from the homepage), largely determined by audience metrics.
  - Editors claimed autonomy in interviews, but in observation metrics were the only consideration routinely invoked.
- **Relevance to theme**: Empirical basis for FAMO mechanism E3. It describes a process in which producers adjust content to metrics, which supports treating E3 as a process rather than a mere practice. It says nothing about polarization outcomes.
- **Keywords**: Audience; case study; field theory; gatekeeping; journalism; web analytics
