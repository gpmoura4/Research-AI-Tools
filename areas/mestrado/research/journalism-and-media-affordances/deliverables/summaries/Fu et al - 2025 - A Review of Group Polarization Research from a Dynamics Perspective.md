# A Review of Group Polarization Research from a Dynamics Perspective

- **Citation key**: Fu_2025
- **Objective**: To review group polarization research from a dynamics perspective, covering definitions, explanatory theories, dynamics models, measurement, and interventions.
- **Method**: Systematic literature review following Kitchenham and Charters, with searches in Scopus, Web of Science, EBSCOhost, and IEEE Xplore (306 records after deduplication, 143 included).
- **Key findings**:
  - Definitions evolved from choice shift (unipolar) to multi-group opposition (bipolar and multipolar polarization).
  - Six explanatory theories coexist; persuasive argument and social comparison remain the dominant pair.
  - Polarization is modeled through propagation dynamics, opinion dynamics, and coupled models; several measurement indices are compared.
  - Interventions target individual opinions, evolutionary mechanisms, or network structure, but most remain at the simulation level and need empirical validation.
- **Relevance to theme**: Supports the FAMO description of group polarization and of filter bubble versus echo chamber, and provides an intervention framework relevant to the affordance matrix and the call for intervention studies.
- **Keywords**: group polarization; dynamics; social media; information dissemination; opinion interaction
