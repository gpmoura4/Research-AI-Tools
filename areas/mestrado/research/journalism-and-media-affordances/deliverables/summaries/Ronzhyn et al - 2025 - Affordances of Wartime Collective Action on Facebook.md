# Affordances of Wartime Collective Action on Facebook

- **Citation key**: Ronzhyn_2025
- **Objective**: To examine how organizational and individual Facebook profiles actualize platform affordances to organize collective action during the war in Ukraine.
- **Method**: Mixed-methods analysis of 3,892 posts from eight large fundraising profiles (CrowdTangle, February–November 2022), with short interviews with managers of three profiles.
- **Key findings**:
  - Organizational profiles actualize persistence and association (transparency), while celebrity profiles actualize visibility and augmenting (authenticity) and obtain higher engagement.
  - Engagement metrics are treated as behavioral indicators of affordance actualization.
  - All interviewed profiles reported shadow banning, content removal, and algorithmic visibility penalties, leading to self-censorship.
  - The authors distinguish affordances from their actualization, criticizing prior work that conflates them.
- **Relevance to theme**: Supports the FAMO distinction between available and enacted affordances and shows platform governance constraining the actualization of other affordances.
- **Keywords**: affordances; collective action; Facebook; affordance actualization; platform governance; Ukraine
